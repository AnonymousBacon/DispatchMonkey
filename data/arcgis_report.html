What the model doesSpreads each corridor's official daily truck count across 24 h with a published profile, then feeds the backlog/queue engine that scores Leave-Now vs Leave-Later.
What the AI does — and doesn'tNo LLM generates or predicts any number. All figures come from this deterministic engine. Gemma 4 via Ollama is planned only to narrate the pre-computed receipt.
Honest label: the curve shape is from published research, not from telemetry we collected. Driver check-ins in the app are the mechanism that turns this into measured, per-fleet hourly data.
